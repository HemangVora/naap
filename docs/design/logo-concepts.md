# NaAP logo concepts

**Idea:** NaAP = New Agent Assessment Programme. Our failure mode is agents *asleep at the wheel*. The yellow/black crash-test roundel, the sticker on every dummy's head, *is* the agent's face.

| Concept | Idea | At 16 px | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| **B · dozing roundel** (`b-v2.svg`) | Crash-test roundel = a sleeping face. The right eye flips to yellow where it crosses the black quarter. Antenna = agent. Snot bubble = asleep | Reads as a roundel plus a bubble | **Primary icon / favicon** |
| **D · airbag pillow** (`d-v2.svg`) | The dozing head sleeps on an inflated airbag (Sekisho) sprouting from the wheel; the wheel arcs read as arms hugging it | Mushy | **Hero illustration** (splash, README, slides) |
| F · sleepy car | Car front: headlights are closed eyelids, snoring grille, hazard bumper crumpled | OK | Fun alternate, says "car" more than "agent" |
| A · asleep at the wheel | Head slumped on a 3-spoke wheel | Reads as a target or eye | Dropped |
| Lockup (`b-lockup-on-*.svg`) | "NaAP" with the lowercase **a** as a tiny dozing roundel | Face too small below 32 px | Use at ≥ 48 px height |

**Sticker border (off-white outline):** keeps every mark legible on dark and light backgrounds, like a real crash-test decal.
